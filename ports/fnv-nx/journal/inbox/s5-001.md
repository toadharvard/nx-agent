* **found (S5-boot)** - The vslot trap at 0x00aa7400 has this cause. Class `FUN_00aa7400` is ZeroOverheadHeap; its
  exe vftable is 0x010a2884. Its native vtable `_ZTV12FUN_00aa7400` (synthesized, 14 slots) has a trap stub in every
  slot. The decomp names those virtuals `FUN_00aa7400::v0..v13`, and no unit defines them. The functions do exist:
  - exe slot 2 is 0x00aa7530, whose native is `ZeroOverheadHeap::Allocate(unsigned, unsigned)` (`__fnv_00aa7530`);
  - slot 3 is Deallocate (0x00aa75e0), slot 4 ContainsBlockImpl (0x00aa75f0);
  - slots 6, 7 and 13 have natives too.
  - The link binds slots by the class's own name, so a function the decomp names under another class
    (ZeroOverheadHeap rather than FUN_00aa7400) never reaches the slot.
* **measured (S5-boot)** - Static count on the guest of link/31971038913d against the owner's exe, over 2,598 V rows:
  - 9,617 trap slots have a native at the exe's slot address; 828 have none.
  - Compiled (non-synthesized) vtables have GCC's slot order, not the exe's. A virtual destructor takes two slots
    (D1/D2 + D0) where MSVC has one, so the slots after it are the exe's k-1. Example: `_ZTV14BGSHungerStage`,
    D2/D0 at 4/5, native 19 = exe 18 (MarkAsModified), native 50 = exe 49.
  - The exe folds identical functions (0x00483710 serves many slots). Another function in the exe's slot is
    therefore no evidence of misalignment; a function found in a nearby exe slot is.
