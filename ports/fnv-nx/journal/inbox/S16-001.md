* **decided** - The metric and threshold (S16 decision; README "Metric and threshold").
  - The metric: frames over the threshold out of all frames compared, mean and max absolute 8-bit error, and the
    same two numbers for the noise floor. A frame the candidate did not render counts as over.
  - Noise floor: two reference runs at `LP_NUM_THREADS` 4 and 1. Measured **0**, bit-identical on every frame.
  - Threshold: a frame passes at PSNR >= 40 dB, SSIM >= 0.98, FLIP <= 0.05, and at most 0.5 % of pixels off by
    more than 8/255. Bounds widen only by a non-zero floor.
  - Permutation captures also get a conditioning floor: the reference re-rendered with every float input scaled by
    1 + 1e-5 u. A frame whose reference moves under it is "ill", which is not evidence.
  - Frames that drew nothing are "vacuous", and are never counted as coverage.

* **found (for the render machine)** - Two defects explain all 11 frames over. Repro for each:
  `perm.py build --sdp shaderpackage019.sdp --only <hash>`, then `replay.py` on both sides (README "The loop").
  - **R1, translator, `render/shaders/mojoshader.patch` `make_GLSL_srcarg_string`.** A relative constant read
    through a CTAB array base (`arg->relative_array != NULL`) is emitted as `uniforms_vec4[ARRAYBASE_n + a0]`,
    without the `dk_relc` overlay of def'd registers. When `a0` runs past the array's CTAB size onto a def'd
    register, D3D9 returns the literal and we return the app's value.
    - WineD3D returns the literal. The synthetic scene `reldef` shows both sides agree on the `dk_relc` path.
    - DISTTREE.vso (`6e1aee01b4089822`, vs_1_1): `InstanceData` c9..c10, reads `c[10 + a0]` with a0 = 1, so c11,
      which is def'd. Max 191, 5.5 % of pixels.
    - PARTICLE.vso (`0f7baa92d5d06448`, vs_2_0): `InstanceData` c18..c19, reads `c[18 / 19 + a0]` with a0 up
      to 3, so c21/c22, the def'd sincos constants. Max 147.
    - Proposed fix: take the `dk_relc` path for every relative constant read in a shader that has def'd constants
      at or above the array base. Or bound `relative_array` reads to the array and fall back to `dk_relc`.
    - Whether the game's own inputs reach past these arrays is not known yet. The real-frame captures will tell.
  - **R2, nx_render D3D9 path: cube textures are sampled as none** (`render/native/capture/d3d9_exec.cpp:477`,
    "cube: unsupported"). This is documented, but here it is measured on the game's programs: 9 of the 11 pixel
    programs with a `samplerCUBE` fail.
    - The 9: SLS1001, SLS1007, SLS1029, SLS1032, SLS1033, SLS2057, SLS2059, SM3024, SM3026. Mean abs up to 9.6;
      up to 58 % of pixels over 8.
    - These are environment and reflection maps. The other two cube programs (SLS2058, SM3025) pass; why
      is not checked yet.
    - Needs: cube `TEX` (flags 2; level bytes = 6 faces in D3DCUBEMAP_FACES order, README "NXD9 extensions")
      uploaded and sampled.
  - The remaining 793 pass, about 1-LSB rounding at most. Nothing else differs between WineD3D and nx_render.

    - Until then S16 normalizes the captures (`tools/compare/nxd9norm.py`: zero texels of the right size), and
      both sides read the same file.
