* **did (item 2, slot fill)** - `fnvlink.exe_slot_fill`, after the pairing. A slot that calls a trap stub gets
  `__fnv_<a>`, where a is the exe vftable's word at that slot (minus-one rule).
  - One symbol per object. Its vtables must agree, else the declaring class's vtable decides.
  - ABI rule `slot_abi` (AArch64 locations): the slot's arguments arrive where the definition reads them.
  - A definition that reads more arguments than the slot declares is bound and listed, because callers use their own
    class's declaration. Example: FUN_00aa7400 `virtual void v2();` = ZeroOverheadHeap::Allocate(unsigned, unsigned).
  - FUN_00aa7400 v1-v4, v6, v7 and v13 are bound. v5 (0x00aa7830) is not compiled.
