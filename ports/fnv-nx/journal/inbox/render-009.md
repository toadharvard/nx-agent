* **found** - Measured: instructions per draw per family, main + render, are 1.6-3.3 k; per frame they are main
  2.34-5.64 M and render 3.36-5.43 M (low/high mix).

  | per frame, IPC 0.6-1.0, caches not modelled | main | render |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1.02 GHz, low mix | 2.3-4.2 ms | 3.3-5.5 ms |
  | 1.02 GHz, high mix | 5.5-10.0 ms | 5.3-8.9 ms |
  | 1.785 GHz, low mix | 1.3-2.5 ms | 1.9-3.1 ms |
  | 1.785 GHz, high mix | 3.2-6.1 ms | 3.0-5.1 ms |

  The renderer alone passes 30/45/60 fps at both clocks (worst case 10.0 ms). Engine update, cull and accumulation are
  not counted. Against the RENDER_NATIVE.md §9.4 budget lines it is over at 1.02 GHz: packet build 5.5-10 ms against
  4.0 / 3.0 ms, render thread 5.3-8.9 ms against 4.5 / 3.5 ms.
* **found** - The "3-5x faster than Nine" claim does not hold in the layer measured. Nine's state tracker costs about the
  same as the native front: 0.70-1.56x per draw by family and 0.69-0.92x per frame (native's fixed sort cost). Not
  measured on the Nine side: the nvc0 driver (needs a GPU) and the engine's own D3D9 path. Any large native win must
  come from those layers; console measurement is pending.
* **found** - GPU model (not measured: uam stats of 30 DKSH x coverage x overdraw, Tegra X1 768/384 MHz):
  - high mix: docked 720p 7.4-12.4 ms, docked 1080p 15.7-26.2 ms, handheld 720p 14.9-24.8 ms, handheld 540p
    9.0-15.1 ms;
  - docked 720p and handheld 540p pass 30/45/60 fps, while handheld 720p and docked 1080p reach 45/60 only at peak
    efficiency, which supports dynamic resolution (render-007).
* **found** - Defects and costs in S6n's front, all sent to S6n:
  - the 8 MiB UBO ring holds about 3,640 draws/frame, so at 3 frames in flight the high mix drops 74 draws;
  - the 16-bit radix sort costs about 1.9 M fixed instructions per frame;
  - fixed 512 B + 256 B constant slices per draw;
  - samplers re-translated on every draw (1.05 M instr/frame);
  - about 120 of 131 instructions of the simple fragment shaders are the runtime D3D9 epilogue;
  - `kMaxPasses` = 16 while the mix uses 15.

  The estimated saving is 2.5-3 M instructions per high frame.
