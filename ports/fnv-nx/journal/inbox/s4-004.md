The backends the game needs right after the menu: music and sound, pads, saves. Metric: services implemented really,
as opposed to stubs, with qemu tests.

* **measured** - what the exe uses, found from its import directory, the COM CLSIDs in the image, and the decomp:
  - sound: DirectSoundCreate8, with buffers, 3D and I3DL2 reverb;
  - music: DirectShow CLSID_FilterGraph (0x00830fd5), the only CLSID in the image; the music files are MP3
    (Data\Music\Special\MainTitle.mp3 at the menu);
  - pads: XINPUT1_3 #2 XInputGetState and #3 XInputSetState; keyboard and mouse go through DirectInput8;
  - saves: SHGetFolderPathA plus the CRT's rename (0x00ec862c) and _access (0x00ecbf05).
  The exe has no XAudio2 (no CLSID, no string), so there is nothing to implement there.

  - minimp3 (CC0, pinned in tools/fetch.sh) decodes it;
  - a pump thread streams it into a looping mixer voice, which goes to host audren;
  - it implements Run/Pause/Stop, Duration and seeks, volume and balance, and EC_COMPLETE on the event handle.
  Test: s7 test_dshow.cpp, host and qemu. It plays our own tone.mp3 and checks pitch per channel, volume, pause,
