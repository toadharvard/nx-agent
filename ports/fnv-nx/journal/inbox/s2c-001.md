* **WIP (S2c-vt, item 1)** - The vftables of the player's exe are paired by RTTI class. This is committed and has not
  been run.
  - `tools/link/exevft.py` scans FalloutNV.exe for (COL, vftable) pairs: TypeDescriptor, COL offset, hierarchy and
    slots. The scan takes 0.8 s and finds 3,269 vftables: 2,678 primary, 591 secondary, 815 templates.
  - `fnvlink.py resolve --exe <exe|none>` (default FNVNX_EXE, then PLAYER_EXE) names each class with llvm-undname
    (`??_7<td>6B@`). `exe_pair` replaces a V pairing with the class's own `_ZTV` at the address point whose
    offset-to-top is -COL offset. It keeps the old pairing only when the class's own vtable is shorter than both the
    old one and the exe's slot count. It also adds V rows for vftables no unit names, and writes `exe_vftables.tsv`
    plus `exe-*` counts in the vtables stats.
  - `exe_requests` asks for synthesis of RTTI classes that have no native vtable (why `exe-rtti`). Hosts are the
    compiled units naming the address, then those naming a member, at most 8.
  - `vtables.slots_at` is new.
