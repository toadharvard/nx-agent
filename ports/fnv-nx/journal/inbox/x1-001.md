* **study** - x86 fallback through FEXCore (FEX-Emu, MIT): `docs/X86_FALLBACK.md`.
* **verdict** - GO-with-limits.
  - Cold code only: the main thread is already over the 60 fps budget (22.9 M against 13.6 M instructions).
  - A load-time vcall rewrite for the Itanium minus-one vtables (D7/D26).
  - Functions with EH frames or virtual bases are not eligible.
  - The JIT needs a FEX RW/RX split patch and CodeMemory svcs in our NPDM. A retail-like environment needs AOT.
* **effort** - 29-42 agent-days in 7 parts (doc section 7).
* **evidence**
  - The memory model is compatible: ILP32 MSVC layouts, the data image at its original VAs, the free .text window,
    the fake TEB with the TLS array, and the IAT holding native functions.
  - FEXCore takes a 6-method SyscallHandler.
  - It has non-Linux embedders: WOW64 (an IA-32 guest) and ARM64EC (mixed native and x86 code).
  - Guest memory is identity mapped.
  - It is clang-only, about 96 k lines.
* **PoC** - `tools/x86fallback/poc_interop.py`, a design stand-in with no exe bytes.
  - Unicorn x86-32 and ARM64 share the same host buffers.
  - It runs a native->x86 `__thiscall` entry and an x86->native `__stdcall` exit through the per-function table.
  - 2000/2000 cases are equal to the all-native path.
  - Real FEX and the exe leaf are the first steps, as farm jobs. The fetch of the private artifacts branches
    stalled for more than 10 min in this container.
* **metric** - n/a (feasibility study; no metric of the stream yet).
* **for the orchestrator** - The first 3 steps are in doc section 9. Step 2 needs a new farm job, `fex_job.sh`.
