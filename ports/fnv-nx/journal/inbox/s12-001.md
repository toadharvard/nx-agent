* **did (S12)** - The whole-program fix of J-0134. The fnvnx_order plugin now orders, in every unit, every field and
  global that any unit of the program hands to Interlocked* or polls in a wait-only loop (docs/ORDERING.md
  "Whole-program keys").
  - `2c8572a`:
    - Interlocked operands are also found through forwarders: reference and pointer wrappers, and wrappers of
      wrappers.
    - POLL-PLAIN: a loop whose condition only reads plain fields or globals and whose body only waits. Its load
      becomes `ldar`, and the stores in every unit become `stlr`.
    - `discover=<file>` writes a unit's keys, also under `-fsyntax-only`.
    - compile.sh defaults to `ORDER_KEYS=<obj dir>/order.keys`. It keeps that list current from a syntax-only pass
      over all units, so a shard sees the same list as the full build.
    - `ORDER_KEYS=unit` is for A/B runs only. A missing `ORDER_KEYS=<file>` is an error.
    - New farm job `tools/farm/keys_job.sh` (runner job `keys`) publishes `k/<commit12>` (order.keys, meta with tree
      hash and inputs hash).
    - compile_job.sh takes that list when the hashes match (`KEYS_WAIT`, default 30 min), or computes it itself. The
      list goes into FLAGS_HASH, so every shard compiles with the same list.
  - `2e2e9f9`: ORDER-OBJECT and ORDER-OPERATOR are switchable: `-fplugin-arg-fnvnx_order-object=first|last` and
    `operator=cpp17|rtl`.
    - The defaults keep today's order. Objects are byte-identical with and without the arguments.
    - `tests/s12/order/evalorder.cpp` has 11 cases and is plain C++03.
